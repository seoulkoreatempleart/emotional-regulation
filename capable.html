My Emotional Regulation

⚡ When I'm Overloaded

I'm tired / hungry / stressed / overwhelmed...

My thoughts may be more convincing right now, but that doesn't mean they're more accurate. Don't make the situation worse. Don't react. Don't make major decisions. Do the next necessary thing. Rest. Then reassess.
